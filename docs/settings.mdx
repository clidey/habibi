# Settings

Open Settings from the bottom of the left sidebar or choose Preferences from the
Habibi menu. Preferences are stored on your Mac.

![Settings with fictional local configuration](/screenshots/preferences.png)

## Appearance

Choose light or dark mode and a theme. **Paper & Ink** is the default. Older
Clidey Ink selections migrate to it automatically. A theme changes the shared
palette while keeping the same product controls and navigation.

## Launcher shortcut and menu bar

Choose **Launcher shortcut**, start recording, and press the key combination you
want to use. Habibi checks availability before saving it. Avoid a combination
that another launcher or a macOS feature is already using.

**Menu bar → Show menu bar icon** applies immediately and persists across
launches. When the icon is hidden, use your configured shortcut or open
Habibi.app from Applications to bring the launcher back.

## Search

**Web search → Default search engine** selects the engine used by Habibi,
including Google, DuckDuckGo, and Bing. This is separate from your browser's own
search preference.

**Local search** shows index health and lets you refresh it. See
[Search and files](/guides/search-and-files) for the indexed locations and limits.

## Home, startup, and accounts

Use **Home layout** to select the briefing, calendar, recent mail, suggestions,
and keyboard footer. The minimal Home setting keeps the page quiet until useful
context is available.

**Start Habibi at login** controls the macOS login registration. Connected
services provide access to model and account setup. Product analytics has its
own toggle; the data contract is explained in [Privacy](/privacy).
