# Your first session

## Open the launcher

Press **⌥ Space** (**Alt Space** on Windows and Linux) or click the Habibi menu
bar or tray icon. The first launch also opens the launcher once on its own. If
another app owns the shortcut, launch Habibi again and record a different
shortcut in **Settings → Launcher shortcut**.

Type an app name, a filename, or a request. Use the arrow keys to choose a result,
**Enter** to open it, and **Escape** to go back or close the launcher. The left
sidebar opens workspaces directly; hover an icon to see its label.

## Try a tool without connecting an account

| Type | What opens |
| --- | --- |
| `Notes` | The notes editor and your page list |
| `Emoji` | A searchable emoji picker with keyboard navigation and copying |
| `Teleprompter` | A script editor and a practice script |
| `Focus timer` | A local focus session |
| `Recent files` | Items opened or previewed through Habibi |

## Connect context when needed

Open [Mail](/guides/mail), [Calendar](/guides/calendar), or
[WhatsApp](/guides/whatsapp) to configure that connection. On macOS, the system
may ask for permission when a capability such as Calendar, Reminders, or the
microphone first needs it; those permissions can be changed later in **System
Settings → Privacy & Security**. On Windows and Linux, connect a Google or
CalDAV account for calendars and reminders.

To ask questions with a model, configure [Ask Habibi](/guides/ask-habibi).
Connecting a model is optional for notes, search, the focus timer, and other
local tools.

## Make the window feel right

In [Settings](/settings), choose light or dark mode, a theme, your search engine,
and the amount of context shown on Home. You can also hide the menu bar or tray
icon; your shortcut and launching Habibi again still bring up the launcher.
