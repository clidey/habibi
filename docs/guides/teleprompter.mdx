# Teleprompter

Type **Teleprompter** in the launcher, paste your script or use the included
example, then choose **Start** or press **⌘ Enter** on a Mac. The launcher steps aside
and the reader attaches to the top edge of the current display.

![Teleprompter editor with a fictional script](/screenshots/teleprompter-editor.png)

## Follow your voice

On macOS and Windows, **Follow my voice** is the default. Allow microphone
access (and speech recognition on macOS) when prompted, then read naturally.
Spoken words fade as the reader follows your place. It holds still during
silence.

Recognition runs on your computer. Audio is not saved or sent to a server. On
Linux, voice following is not available, and on any system where on-device
recognition is unavailable for the system language, use automatic scrolling
instead.

## Scroll automatically

Choose **Automatic scrolling** and set the reading pace. The editor also lets
you adjust text size and reader width. **Load example** restores the practice
script so you can test the reader without using private content.

![Floating reader with fictional text](/screenshots/teleprompter-overlay.png)

## Control the reader

Use its controls to restart, pause, resume, and close. When the reader is focused,
**Space** pauses or resumes and **Escape** closes it.

Your script and reading preferences are saved locally. Pausing, closing,
or finishing the script stops listening and scrolling. No model
connection is required. See [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting) for microphone
or display issues.
